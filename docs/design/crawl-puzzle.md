# Low crawl and deterministic puzzle prototype

Owner direction, 5 November 2026. REQ-030 / MS-014 / TC-034.

Z toggles low crawl. Ctrl/C retains held crouch. Crawl has a 36 cm tall capsule, 30 cm eye, 18 cm shot target and 85 cm/s movement. Jump and sprint cannot raise or accelerate a crawler. Leaving crawl performs an actual clearance test; blocked transitions retain the full crawl state. The same posed human transfers into death physics.

The default launcher selects puzzle recipe 1, seed 71031. Heights are generated by a pure bounded height function on a 50 cm triangle grid. The exact same triangles render and collide; no flat floor beneath the playable hills. The protected starting area is level. Broad low hills, shallow routes and dispersed single-body shelters replace the central pile. The turret muzzle is 30 cm above its local ground. Cover is silhouette-dependent: one body works from its designated close shelter, not from every angle or distance. Open ground remains dangerous even when prone.

Initial bodies are settled once with Chaos in a development bake, then their local bone poses and world mesh transforms are versioned. Normal play loads these poses into immutable cover; it never reruns the random physics solve. Seed and recipe version must match. Missing data fails visibly in logs instead of silently generating a different puzzle. Player deaths still simulate and persist for the current scene. R retries do not reconstruct terrain or starting bodies. Cross-session saves of player deaths remain deferred.

Acceptance checks: pure repeatable height/layout rules and bounded gradients; actual terrain trace agreement; stance dimensions/speed, blocked expansion and input; one initial body blocks an actual turret ray while adjacent open ground takes damage; route waypoints traversed by CharacterMovement without teleporting between them; two launches yield identical initial pose/collision fingerprints; retry keeps poses and IDs; human death pose transfer; packaged start/overhead/crawl/cover screenshots. Record startup timing and limits, not an unmeasured performance claim.
