# Persistent ragdoll deaths

User-directed continuation of the first playable, 2 November 2026. REQ-002, REQ-003, REQ-026; TC-030; partial MS-006, MS-007, MS-009 and MS-011. [ADR 0006](../engineering/adr/0006-persistent-ragdolls.md) supersedes the earlier instant reset and death presentation timing.

The later [crouch/settling refinement](crouch-and-settling.md) replaces the earlier 0.75 s lock with supported collapse and sustained quiet, independently of camera return. Moving meat also blocks bullets.

On the first lethal hit for a life, create one articulated human proxy at the actual death position with the player's yaw and sampled movement velocity. Use native Chaos rigid bodies and joints to let the body topple, with a shot impulse. The first-person avatar becomes hidden/noncolliding and movement/look is temporarily held while an independent camera pulls behind and above the falling corpse. Initial timing: 0.35 s pull-back, 0.35 s observation and 0.35 s return to the starting line. The world and turret timers continue. Return healthy in first person at the protected start after 1.05 s, with an acceptance limit of 1.2 s. R during this transition cannot spawn a duplicate or bypass its transaction.

Generate the body procedurally from fitted primitive physics parts and joints. This is an articulated proxy mannequin, not final skeletal character art. Falling bodies block visibility/weapon queries but ignore the player and mutual active-rig physics contacts. They collide with the ground, authored cover and older committed bodies; older bodies cannot be displaced. After the settle conditions pass, capture every body's world pose, stop its simulation, and create separate fixed collision primitives matching that pose. The visible body then remains still and the fixed geometry owns movement, bullet and sight collision.

Keep every corpse actor and its visible pose through every later life and manual R retry in this loaded level. No time/count eviction, silent disappearance or level reload. Quitting/relaunching still starts a fresh demo; save/load is future work. This slice measures preservation and stability; it does not claim the original two-primitives-per-body budget or a completed construction placement/performance gate. The earlier bounded placement proposal remains a future design question where it conflicts with actual frozen ragdoll poses.

Tests must cover duplicate lethal requests, invalid time, exact camera phase boundaries, one completion event, no duplicate restart, actual physics movement, stable committed transforms, collision/sight interception, a second independent death, old-body persistence through that death and R, safe controllable return and bounded temporary camera actors. Review rendered fall/return and both corpses in the packaged game. Preserve deterministic clocks and explicit corpse/life IDs; physics fixtures use explicit starting velocity and impulse, with tolerances rather than claiming cross-run bit-identical Chaos poses.

In the open-lane fixture the observation camera must clear the avatar capsule and show the whole body from at least 2.2 m away. The committed torso must tilt out of its upright pose and fall below 0.8 m above the floor; translation alone is not proof of a falling ragdoll.
